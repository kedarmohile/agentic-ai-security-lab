# Pricing Strategy

AASL uses a local-first commercial model so unlimited learning does not imply unlimited cloud-compute liability.

## Recommended launch plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Positioning |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Free | $0 | $0 | Explore AASL |
| Core | $9 | $89 | Full learning + local practice |
| Pro | $29 | $249 | Core + bounded hosted labs |
| Teams | Custom | Custom | Managed organizational readiness |

Annual equivalents:

- Core annual: about **$7.42/month**, roughly 18% below paying $9 monthly for twelve months.
- Pro annual: **$20.75/month**, roughly 28% below paying $29 monthly for twelve months.

## Market position

Current individual cyber-learning markets commonly span:

- free entry tiers;
- roughly low-teens monthly-equivalent annual plans for core learning;
- roughly mid-$20s to around $40/month for advanced hands-on environments;
- higher custom pricing for enterprise readiness platforms.

AASL intentionally places:

- **Core below typical advanced cyber-learning subscriptions**;
- **Pro inside the advanced hands-on range** while adding a specialized GenAI / Agentic AI focus.

No competitor links are shown on the pricing page.

## Free

Designed to create trust and product discovery.

Intended entitlement:

- selected Academy missions;
- selected reference labs;
- deterministic local answer evaluation;
- browser voice where supported;
- local simulations and evidence;
- open-source local console.

## Core — $9/month or $89/year

Designed for the majority of individual learners.

Intended entitlement:

- full Academy;
- all Career Lab role tracks;
- Interview Studio;
- Simulation Range;
- GenAI mastery evaluation under fair-use limits;
- interviewer personas and browser voice;
- local Multipass VM labs;
- Target Lab for local/GitHub projects;
- progress, evidence, retries, and mastery history;
- unlimited learning content and local practice.

"Unlimited" here means content and local practice, not unmetered cloud compute.

## Pro — $29/month or $249/year

Designed for learners who need hosted environments.

Intended entitlement:

- everything in Core;
- one concurrent hosted lab;
- 30 hosted hours/month on monthly billing;
- 300 hosted hours/year on annual billing;
- unlimited lab launches within the compute allowance;
- browser terminal;
- clean reset/recreate;
- advanced hosted GenAI / Agentic AI ranges;
- hosted OWASP + MITRE ATLAS mission environments;
- TTL and idle shutdown.

Hosted lab infrastructure is still a roadmap capability; the price is documented now so the product can be designed around a sustainable compute envelope.

## Teams

Roadmap plan for organizations.

Potential capabilities include:

- managed seats;
- assigned learning paths;
- readiness dashboards;
- private missions;
- organization-specific content;
- SSO;
- managed ranges;
- private target integrations.

These should not be sold as generally available until implemented.

## Cost guardrails

For hosted labs:

- one active environment per Pro learner;
- hard TTL;
- idle shutdown;
- annual/monthly compute allowance;
- automatic cleanup;
- per-session cost attribution;
- no indefinitely persistent environment by default.

This keeps Pro economically predictable while preserving the user experience of being able to launch labs whenever needed.
